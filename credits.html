BÍN: Database of Icelandic Morphology

Beygingarlýsing íslensks nútímamáls. Stofnun Árna Magnússonar í íslenskum fræðum. Höfundur og ritstjóri Kristín Bjarnadóttir.

Every inflected form in TÖLVA comes from the DMII Core (BÍN-kjarninn) of the Árni Magnússon Institute for Icelandic Studies, bin.arnastofnun.is. License: CC BY-SA 4.0. Paradigms are shown exactly as published, without changes. Seven indeclinable numerals that the DMII Core lacks were confirmed in the full BÍN through Ylhýra.

Wiktionary

Glosses, IPA, etymology and Norwegian translations come from Wiktionary by its contributors, extracted by kaikki.org (wiktextract). License: CC BY-SA 4.0.

Tatoeba

Example sentences and translations come from Tatoeba. License: CC BY 2.0 FR. Each sentence in the app links to its page and names its author. Every sentence was checked with GreynirCorrect before use.

Recordings

Word recordings are played from Wikimedia Commons, most of them made with Lingua Libre. Each recording shows its speaker and license where it is played.

Generated voice

Words and sentences without a human recording are spoken by the voice Steinn, generated ahead of time with Piper (GPL-3.0; a build tool, not part of the website). The Icelandic Piper voices were trained on the Talrómur corpus by Reykjavík University (CC BY 4.0). The generated audio is offered under CC BY 4.0 with that credit; sentence audio speaks Tatoeba sentences, credited above. Compare all four voices.

CLARIN-IS frequency lists

The order of the course vocabulary follows "Frequency lists for Icelandic 23.06", hdl.handle.net/20.500.12537/314. License: CC BY 4.0.

Tools

Sentences are checked with GreynirCorrect by Miðeind (MIT license) when the data is built. It is not part of the website.

Code: MIT license. Generated data in /data: CC BY-SA 4.0 with the attributions above (see data/LICENSE.md and data/SOURCES.json). The mascot Tala, the icons and the design are original work for TÖLVA. Fonts: system fonts only, no font services.
